Every theme park in the United States will now be required to show a two-minute safety video before guests board any roller coaster, featuring security footage of Gary Plunkett, a 900-pound man from Ocala who, after being told his lap bar would not close, assured a teenage ride operator, "Don't worry, I'll just hold on real tight."

The video, titled *Holding On Is Not a Restraint System*, was produced by the International Association of Amusement Parks in partnership with ride manufacturers, insurers, and what one producer described as "basically every lawyer in Florida."

The footage shows Plunkett attempting to lower the lap bar of the Vortex Falcon coaster at Sunshine Kingdom Adventure Park. The bar stops roughly 14 inches above where it is designed to lock. The ride operator, 17-year-old Brayden Cole, can be heard on the audio saying, "Uh, sir, I don't think it's locked."

Plunkett responds, "It's fine, man. I'll just hold on real tight. I've got strong arms."

Cole then checks his phone, glances at the line of 300 waiting guests, and says, "Okay, cool," before pressing the dispatch button.

The video does not show what happens next, cutting instead to a black screen with white text reading: *He did not have strong enough arms.* The screen then fades to a diagram explaining g-forces, followed by a cartoon of a stick figure holding a lap bar with an expression of growing concern.

"We wanted the video to be clear but tasteful," said producer Lisa Martinez. "We don't need to show the outcome. Everyone in the audience knows. The arms were not strong enough. They never are."

The video concludes with a final message: *Lap bars must click. Hope is not a click.*

Park visitors report that the video has had a sobering effect. "Everybody gets real quiet," said one guest in Atlanta. "Then a big guy behind me said, 'Well, that guy was just weak. I'd hold on.' The whole line turned around and looked at him."

At press time, Sunshine Kingdom had added a new sign to the Vortex Falcon queue reading: *No, You Will Not Just Hold On Real Tight.*
